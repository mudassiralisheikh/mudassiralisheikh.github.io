M Channel Uploader

A private publishing tool for the YouTube channel Mudassar Ali (@themudassarali).

M Channel Uploader is a desktop script that Mudassar Ali runs on his own computer to publish his own videos to his own YouTube channel. It uploads finished episodes and Shorts and sets their title, description, chapters, tags, thumbnail, subtitles and playlist. It changes visibility only when he chooses. It also reads his channel's own statistics and comments so he can review how his videos perform.

The tool is not offered to the public. There is no sign-up, and nobody other than the channel owner uses it.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who uses it. Only the owner of the @themudassarali channel. No other user data is collected.

What it accesses. Through Google's YouTube API Services, with the owner's permission, it:

  • uploads and updates videos, thumbnails, captions and playlists on his own channel;
  • reads his own channel's statistics and YouTube Analytics reports;
  • reads public comments on his own videos.

Where data is kept. The sign-in token and any reports stay on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's, and nothing is sold or shared.

Your rights. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token on the owner's computer removes all stored access.

Google. This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

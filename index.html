M Channel Uploader

A private publishing tool for Mudassar Ali's own channels: YouTube @Mudassir.AliSheikh, Facebook Mudassir Ali Sheikh, Instagram @mudassir.alisheikh and TikTok @mudassir.alisheikh.

M Channel Uploader is a desktop script that Mudassar Ali runs on his own computer to publish his own videos. On YouTube it uploads finished episodes and Shorts and sets their title, description, chapters, tags, thumbnail, subtitles and playlist, and it changes visibility only when he chooses. It also reads his channel's own statistics and comments so he can review how his videos perform. On Instagram it publishes his reels with their captions. On TikTok it sends his short videos to his own account.

The tool is not offered to the public. There is no sign-up, and nobody other than the channel owner uses it.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who uses it. Only Mudassar Ali, on his own accounts: YouTube @Mudassir.AliSheikh, the Facebook Page Mudassir Ali Sheikh, Instagram @mudassir.alisheikh and TikTok @mudassir.alisheikh. No other user data is collected.

What it accesses. With the owner's permission, it:

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): uploads and updates videos, thumbnails, captions and playlists on his own channel; reads his own channel's statistics, YouTube Analytics reports and the public comments on his own videos.
  • Instagram (Instagram API): publishes his own reels to his own account and reads his own account's basic profile and publishing limit.
  • Facebook (Pages API): publishes his own reels to his own Page and reads the Page's basic details.
  • TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API): reads his basic profile (display name, avatar, open ID) and sends his own videos to his TikTok inbox as drafts.

Where data is kept. Sign-in tokens and any reports stay on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's, Meta's and TikTok's own APIs, and nothing is sold or shared.

Data retention and deletion. No data about other people is stored. The owner's tokens are kept only while he uses the tool and can be deleted at any time. Access can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google/YouTube), in Instagram under Settings > Website permissions, and in TikTok under Settings > Security > Manage app permissions. Revoking access or deleting the tokens removes all stored access.

Google. This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Meta and TikTok. Instagram's privacy policy and TikTok's privacy policy also apply to content published there.
